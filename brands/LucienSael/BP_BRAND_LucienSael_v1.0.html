Construction

Cormorant Garamond 300. "Lucien" in bone, upright. "Sael" in ember, italic. Stacked vertically. Letter-spacing .12em. Line-height .88. The first name grounds. The surname fires.

Color rule

Ember on dark backgrounds (primary). On light backgrounds: carbon upright + ember italic. Gold is never used in the logotype — it belongs to secondary accents only.

Minimum size

16px minimum in digital. Below that, use the inline variant: "Lucien Sael" in a single line, no stacking, tracking .08em.

Clear space

Minimum clear space: 1× the cap-height of "Lucien" on all sides. The logotype breathes — it does not crowd.

Never — stretching or condensing

The logotype is never scaled non-proportionally. The relationship between Lucien and Sael is fixed.

Never — "Sael" in a color other than ember or carbon

Not gold, not parchment, not bone. The surname is always ember (dark BG) or carbon (light BG).

Never — "Lucien" italic

Only "Sael" is italic. "Lucien" is always upright. The asymmetry is the point.

Never — adding tagline to logotype

"I build worlds. Some of them survive." is the signature phrase — it lives below, separated. Never inside or attached to the logotype mark.

The phrase — rules

"I build worlds. Some of them survive." is Lucien's permanent signature phrase. It replaces any professional title or descriptor. It is never attributed to a company, never qualified with context, and never changed. It appears in Crimson Pro italic, in parchment, below the logotype or name. It is the only thing that needs to be said.

05 — Rules of Use
Voice

First person, always. Direct, never corporate. Opinions stated as positions, not suggestions. The tone is someone who has stopped asking for permission.

Do — Colors

Ember for the single most important element per view. Gold for secondary emphasis. Bone for text. Parchment for supporting text. Carbon for surfaces.

Never — Colors

Never use cyan, pure white, or any blue. Those are UNRLVL's world. Lucien's world is ember, gold, and carbon. The two identities are related but never interchangeable.

Relationship to UNRLVL

Lucien Sael is the person. Unreal>ille Studio is one of his worlds. The site mentions UNRLVL as a project — never as an affiliation or employer. Lucien is larger than any one world he builds.

Do — Typography

Cormorant Garamond for anything that commands attention. Crimson Pro for anything that needs to be read. JetBrains Mono for anything that needs to be precise.

Never — Typography

No system fonts. No sans-serif in editorial contexts. No Bebas Neue (UNRLVL). No Space Mono (UNRLVL). The typographic world of Lucien is entirely different from the studio.

Content language

English first, always. Spanish when the subject demands it or the audience is specifically LATAM. No regional Spanish. International only — no Spain, no Mexico, no Argentina. Neutral.

Do — Signature phrase

"I build worlds. Some of them survive." appears in every footer, every byline, every email. It is the one constant. It never changes. It requires no explanation.

Never — Titles or roles

Never "CEO", "Founder", "Strategist", "Consultant". The phrase replaces all titles. If context requires, "builder" or "operator" — lowercase, as descriptors, not titles.
