## English

> **The finishing shine, without weight**  
> The spray Patricia Osorio uses to finish her Moisture & Shine ritual.

The finishing-touch shine spray. It leaves hair luminous and hydrated, even when damaged, with a light, non-greasy formula that adds silkiness.

**Benefits.** instant shine; light, non-greasy formula; silkiness.

**Hot & humid · Florida.** Weightless shine that won't leave hair sticky or greasy in the heat.

**Directions.** Once your style is done and hair is dry, mist evenly from about 30 cm (12 in) away.

**Precautions.** Keep the product out of your eyes. Store it out of children's reach. Should any reaction appear, stop using it.
